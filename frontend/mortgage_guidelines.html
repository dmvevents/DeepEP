📊 Key Income Types & Calculation Methods

Base Salary/Hourly

Calculation: Current monthly amount (or hourly × 40 × 52 / 12)

Documentation: Pay stubs (last 30 days), W-2s (2 years)

Continuity: Must be stable and likely to continue for 3 years

Bonus / Commission / Overtime

Calculation: 2-year average (sum of 24 months / 24)

Documentation: W-2s (2 years), year-end pay stubs or VOE

Trending: If declining, may use lower amount or exclude

Self-Employment Income

Calculation: (Year 1 net + Year 2 net) / 24 months

Documentation: Tax returns (2 years), Schedule C/1120S, P&L statement

Adjustments: Add back depreciation, depletion, one-time expenses

Ownership: 25%+ ownership = self-employed

Rental Income

Calculation: 75% of gross monthly rent (Fannie/Freddie)

Documentation: Lease agreement or Schedule E

Note: Accounts for vacancies and maintenance

🏦 Fannie Mae Conventional Loans

2025 Loan Limits

Standard Areas: $806,500 (single-family home)

High-Cost Areas: Up to $1,209,750

Increase: 5.21% over 2024 limits

Debt-to-Income Requirements

Desktop Underwriter (DU) - Automated

  • Maximum DTI: 50%
  • Can handle higher ratios with compensating factors
  • Risk-based pricing adjusts for DTI levels

Manual Underwriting

  • Standard Maximum: 36%
  • Can Exceed To: 45% with compensating factors:
  • ✓ Credit score ≥ 720
  • ✓ Reserves of 6+ months PITI
  • ✓ Down payment ≥ 20%

Income Calculation Methods

Fannie Mae Income Calculator

Official tool available at singlefamily.fanniemae.com

Enforcement Relief: When lender's qualifying income ≤ Income Calculator amount, may receive rep & warranty relief

Supported Income Types:

  • Base salary/hourly wages
  • Bonus, overtime, commission
  • Self-employment (Schedule C, 1120S, 1065)
  • Rental income
  • Investment/retirement income

Self-Employment Guidelines

Definition

25% or greater ownership in business = Self-employed

Required Documentation

  • Personal tax returns (2 years with all schedules)
  • Business tax returns (2 years - 1120, 1120S, or 1065)
  • YTD Profit & Loss statement
  • Balance sheet (for corporations/partnerships)

Income Calculation

Formula: (Year 1 Net Income + Year 2 Net Income) / 24 months

Add-backs: Depreciation, depletion, amortization, one-time losses

Deductions: Business use of home, meals/entertainment (partially)

Trending Analysis Required: If income declining, use lower or exclude

Bonus/Commission/Overtime Income

Minimum History: 2 years

Calculation Method:

  • Option 1: Average last 2 years (24 months / 24)
  • Option 2: If increasing, may use current year amount with justification

Documentation: W-2s (2 years) + year-end pay stubs OR written VOE

Continuity: Must demonstrate likelihood of continuation

Credit & Reserve Requirements

Minimum Credit Score

620 (typically) - varies by LTV and other risk factors

Reserve Requirements

Property Type Number of Units Required Reserves
Primary Residence 1 0-2 months (varies by LTV/credit)
Primary Residence 2-4 6 months
Investment Property 1 6 months

Debt-to-Income Requirements

Loan Product Advisor (LPA) - Automated

  • Maximum DTI: 43%

Manual Underwriting

  • Maximum DTI: 45%

Income Calculator

Freddie Mac Income Calculator

Free online tool: sf.freddiemac.com/income-calculator

Supported Types:

  • Base, bonus, overtime, commission (from paystubs/W-2s)
  • Self-employment (Schedule C, Partnership, S-Corp)
  • Rental income
  • Retirement/investment income

Part-Time & Fluctuating Income

Non-Fluctuating vs Fluctuating

Non-Fluctuating: Salaried employees with consistent pay

Fluctuating: Commissions, bonuses, overtime, tips, variable hourly

Requirements

  • History: 2-year employment history without interruption
  • Calculation: Average of 24 months for fluctuating income
  • Continuity: Likely to continue for 3+ years

Rental Income

Lease Agreement Method

75% of gross monthly rent

Accounts for vacancies and maintenance expenses

Tax Return Method (Schedule E)

Use actual net rental income from Schedule E

May add back depreciation and other non-cash expenses

Asset Depletion Income

Calculation Formula

70% of investment account balance ÷ 240 months

Result = Qualifying monthly income

Example

$500,000 in investments → ($500,000 × 0.70) / 240 = $1,458/month

Eligible Assets

  • Checking/savings accounts
  • Investment accounts (stocks, bonds, mutual funds)
  • Retirement accounts (IRA, 401k - with restrictions)

Documentation Requirements

IRS Tax Transcripts Required When:

  • Qualifying with self-employment income
  • Using rental income (Schedule E)
  • Employment by family-owned business
  • Grossing up non-taxable income

🏠 FHA Loans (HUD Guidelines)

2025 FHA Loan Limits

Standard Areas: $524,225 (single-family)

High-Cost Areas: Up to $1,209,750

Credit Score Requirements

Credit Score Minimum Down Payment Max LTV
580+ 3.5% 96.5%
500-579 10% 90%
<500 Not Eligible

Debt-to-Income Requirements

Standard DTI Limits

  • Front-End (Housing) Ratio: 31%
  • Back-End (Total) Ratio: 43%

Maximum with Credit Score 580+

  • PTI (Payment-to-Income): Up to 40%
  • DTI (Total Debt): Up to 50%
  • Requires additional qualifications

Absolute Maximum (With Justification)

  • Back-End DTI: Up to 56.99%
  • Requires compensating factors:
  • ✓ Large down payment (20%+)
  • ✓ Significant cash reserves
  • ✓ Excellent credit history
  • ✓ Minimal increase in housing payment

Income Requirements

No Minimum Income

FHA does not set a minimum income requirement

Income must be sufficient to meet DTI ratios

Documentation Requirements

  • Verifiable income through pay stubs, tax returns, bank statements
  • Steady employment history
  • Proof of employment (VOE)

Income Types Accepted

  • W-2 wage income (2-year history)
  • Self-employment income (2-year tax returns)
  • Social Security/disability income
  • Retirement/pension income
  • Alimony/child support (with 3+ years continuance)
  • Part-time income (2-year history)

Self-Employment Income

Documentation Required

  • Personal tax returns (2 years with all schedules)
  • Business tax returns (2 years if applicable)
  • Year-to-date P&L statement
  • CPA letter or business license

Calculation Method

Average of 2 years adjusted gross income from tax returns

Add back non-cash deductions (depreciation, depletion)

If declining trend, use lower amount or may not qualify

Mortgage Insurance

Upfront Mortgage Insurance Premium (UFMIP)

Rate: 1.75% of base loan amount

Can be financed into loan amount

Annual Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP)

Base Loan Amount LTV ≤ 95% LTV > 95%
≤ $726,200 0.50% 0.55%
> $726,200 0.70% 0.75%

Duration: Life of loan (for most borrowers)

🌾 USDA Rural Housing Loans

Income Limits (2025)

Household Income Limits

1-4 Member Household: $119,850

5-8 Member Household: $158,250

Effective June 18, 2025 (likely through 2026)

Calculation Method

Income limits set at 115% of Area Median Income (AMI)

Your annual income cannot exceed 15% above median income for your area

Regional Variations

Most Counties: $112,450 (1-4) / $148,450 (5-8)

Rural Areas: Can be as low as $91,900 / $121,300

Higher Cost Areas: Up to limits shown above

Household Income Definition

What's Included

All Adult Household Members: Income from all adults counted, whether on loan or not

  • W-2 wage income
  • Self-employment income
  • Social Security benefits
  • Retirement/pension income
  • Rental income
  • Alimony/child support received

What's Excluded

  • Income of household members under 18
  • Foster care payments
  • Student financial aid
  • Temporary/seasonal income

Income Deductions

Allowed Adjustments

If your income slightly exceeds limits, you may deduct:

  • Childcare Expenses: Documented annual costs
  • Disability Expenses: Medical costs for disabled household members
  • Elderly Household Expenses: Medical costs for members 62+

These deductions can bring you below the income limit threshold

Debt-to-Income Requirements

Standard DTI Limits

  • Front-End (Housing) Ratio: 29%
  • Back-End (Total) Ratio: 41%

Exceptions with Compensating Factors

May exceed 41% with:

  • Excellent credit history (700+ score)
  • Cash reserves (6+ months)
  • Minimal increase in housing expense
  • Strong employment history

Property Eligibility

Location Requirements

Property must be in USDA-eligible rural area

Check Eligibility: USDA Eligibility Map

Generally Eligible

  • Communities with population < 35,000
  • Rural areas outside metropolitan cities
  • Some suburban areas qualify

Credit Requirements

Minimum Credit Score

Typical: 640

No absolute minimum, but lower scores require manual underwriting

Credit History

  • 12 months payment history on all accounts
  • No bankruptcies in last 3 years
  • No foreclosures in last 3 years

Loan Features

Key Benefits

  • 0% Down Payment required
  • 100% financing available
  • Competitive interest rates
  • 30-year fixed terms

Guarantee Fee

  • Upfront: 1% of loan amount (financed)
  • Annual: 0.35% of loan balance

🎖️ VA Home Loans

Debt-to-Income Requirements

Benchmark DTI Ratio

Standard: 41%

VA allows flexibility above 41% with compensating factors

Compensating Factors for Higher DTI

  • Excellent credit history
  • Down payment (even though not required)
  • Cash reserves
  • Long employment history
  • Minimal increase in housing payment
  • Strong residual income

Residual Income (Unique to VA)

What is Residual Income?

Money left over each month after paying debts and obligations

Formula: Gross Monthly Income - Monthly Expenses

Why It Matters

Residual income must exceed VA chart values to qualify

Ensures veteran has sufficient funds for family necessities

Employment & Income Verification

Employment History

  • Required: 2-year employment history
  • Gaps explained and documented
  • Military service counts toward history

Income Continuity

Lender must determine income is likely to continue through first 3 years of mortgage

Bonus/Overtime Income

  • Must be received for past 2 years
  • Likely to continue
  • Calculate using 2-year average

Part-Time Income

  • 2-year employment history required
  • No interruptions in employment
  • Same or similar part-time work

Credit Requirements

No Minimum Credit Score

VA does not set a minimum credit score

Typical Lender Requirement: 620

Credit History Requirements

  • 12 months satisfactory payment history
  • No late payments in last 12 months
  • Bankruptcy: 2+ years since discharge
  • Foreclosure: 2+ years since completion

VA Loan Features

Key Benefits

  • 0% Down Payment (100% financing)
  • No Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)
  • Competitive interest rates
  • Seller can pay all closing costs
  • Assumption allowed (transferable)

Exemptions: Veterans with service-connected disabilities

Certificate of Eligibility

Who's Eligible

  • Veterans with qualifying service
  • Active-duty service members (after 90 days)
  • National Guard/Reserve (6+ years)
  • Surviving spouses

How to Obtain

Apply online at eBenefits or through lender
